Privacy

How this portfolio handles visitor data · last updated November 2026

What is collected

When you visit this site or play the 3D island, the server records:

  • Your IP address, and the approximate country and city derived from it.
  • Your device type (phone or desktop), the page you visited, and the page that linked you here.
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser, so return visits are counted once.
  • If you play: your score, flags, coins and completion time.

Why

To count visitors, understand where they come from, and run the public scoreboard.

Who can see it

  • Public scoreboard: only an automatically generated name (for example "Neon Falcon"), your country flag and your score. Never your IP address.
  • Private visit log: IP addresses and the other details above are visible only to the site owner.

Storage and retention

Data is stored with the site's hosting providers, Vercel and Upstash. The private log is kept by the site owner with no fixed deletion date. Game progress is also saved in your own browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time.

Your choices

To have your visit data or scoreboard entry removed, email dakshkhurana1222@gmail.com. No cookies or analytics trackers are used.
