How to play

Rules

Each of the 6 areas has a flag. Complete the area's challenge, then drive into the flag to capture it: +100 pts.
13 gold Hall of Fame coins are hidden around the island: +25 pts each.
Capture all 6 flags and the secret root flag appears on Root Hill: +300 pts.
Points level you up and unlock gear: boost, jump, the truck, paint, night mode, and gold ROOT status.
Every flag and coin counts once. The scoreboard ranks by points, then by fastest full clear.
You get a random explorer name and your country's flag automatically. No sign-up. Privacy
